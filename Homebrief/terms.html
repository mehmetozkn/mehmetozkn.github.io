1. Purpose and Usage

Homebrief is a family organizer. It reads school and activity messages, letters, PDFs, photos and group-chat exports you share, and turns the dates, deadlines, payments and things to pack it finds into tasks, reminders and a weekly brief for your family.

  • AI Can Be Wrong: Tasks are found automatically by artificial intelligence. It can miss something, read a date, time or amount wrongly, or link a task to the wrong child. The App shows each task with its source sentence and marks details it isn't sure about so you can check them before saving.
  • Always Check the Original: The original message or document from the school or organizer is what counts. Check important dates, amounts and requirements there, and contact the school if anything is unclear. Homebrief is not affiliated with any school, club or messaging service.
  • Reminders Are a Help, Not a Guarantee: Notifications depend on your device, its settings and iOS, and may be delayed or not delivered. You remain responsible for meeting deadlines and making payments.

2. Payments You Track

The App only lists payments mentioned in the content you share and lets you mark them as paid. It does not make, receive or process any payment to schools or anyone else, and an amount shown in the App is not an invoice.

3. Your Content and Your Family

  • Only Share What You May Share: Only share content you are allowed to share, such as messages and documents addressed to you as a parent. Don't share documents with sensitive information (medical, financial or identity documents) or content about other people that they wouldn't expect to be processed. The App removes sender names and phone numbers from chat exports, but other text is read as it is.
  • AI Processing: Content you share is sent to Google's Gemini models to be read, under Google's terms (see our Privacy Policy). You can always add tasks by hand instead.
  • Family Members: Adults you invite with a code or link can see and change your family's tasks and children. Only invite people you trust, and keep invite codes private.

4. Free Features & Homebrief Pro

  • Free: A limited number of AI reads per month and a limited number of children, plus unlimited tasks added by hand, reminders, the morning list, the weekly brief, sharing with your partner, adding single events to your calendar, and the small widget. The current limits are shown in the App.
  • Homebrief Pro: Unlimited AI reads (subject to fair use below), reading whole group-chat exports, unlimited children, adding to your calendar automatically, and the medium and Lock Screen widgets. One purchase covers the adults in your family. The exact features may change as the App is updated.
  • Fair Use: To keep the service available for everyone, AI reads are limited per family per day and, for Pro and lifetime purchases, to a high monthly number. Content over a limit waits in your Inbox and is read later.
