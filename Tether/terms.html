1. Purpose and Usage

Tether helps two partners who live apart stay close: it shows your partner's local time, weather and status, finds the hours you are both free, offers a daily question to answer together, and lets you send touches, gifts and countdowns.

  • Two People, With Consent: Tether is made for two adults (13+) who both choose to connect. Only invite someone who wants to share their time, status and answers with you. Either partner can end the connection at any time (Settings → Unpair).
  • Not a Tracking or Safety Tool: Tether never shows anyone's location. A partner's status is an estimate based on the schedule they entered, or a status they set themselves; it may be out of date or wrong. Do not use the App to monitor someone, or to judge whether a person is safe. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number.
  • iCloud Required: connecting with a partner requires iCloud on both iPhones. Some features may be delayed by network conditions or iOS limits.

  • If something feels wrong, unpair: this deletes the shared content for both partners.

3. Free Features, Tether Pro and One-Time Purchases

  • Free: connecting with your partner, their local time, weather and status, your next window to talk, the daily question with both answers, recent answers, one countdown, the small widget and one-tap touches.
  • Tether Pro: window alerts, the week view of shared free time, the full question archive, unlimited countdowns, Lock Screen and medium widgets with a touch button, touch patterns and the Style Pack. The exact features may change as the App is updated.
  • Shared Between Partners: while you are connected, one Tether Pro subscription or Lifetime purchase unlocks Pro for both partners. If the partner who paid ends the connection, the other partner loses access to Pro.
  • Lifetime and Style Pack: one-time purchases that unlock Pro, or the extra icons and themes, without a subscription. They can be restored with "Restore purchases" on the same Apple Account.
  • Gifts: virtual coffee, flowers and love letters are consumable one-time purchases delivered to your connected partner. They have no cash value, are not transferable, and cannot be restored. They require a connected partner.
