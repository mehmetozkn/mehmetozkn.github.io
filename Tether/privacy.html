Tether Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Tether ("the App") connects two partners who live apart. It shows your partner's local time, weather and status, finds the hours you are both free to talk, gives you one question to answer together every day, and lets you send touches, gifts and countdowns. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses, where it is kept, who can see it, and how you stay in control.

In short: there is no account. What you share with your partner (your profile, schedule, status, answers, letters and dates) is stored in your own iCloud and shared only with the partner you connect with. We, the developer, cannot read it. We use anonymous analytics and crash reports to improve the App, Apple Weather for the weather, and Apple and RevenueCat for purchases.

1. Information You Enter

  • Your Profile: your first name (or nickname) and an emoji.
  • Your City: a city you pick from a list. The App uses it for your time zone and weather and stores its coordinates rounded to about 1 km. The App never accesses your device's location.
  • Your Schedule and Status: your usual sleep hours, busy hours (for example work or classes), and a status you can set by hand ("Can talk now", "Busy", "Do not disturb").
  • Shared Content: your answers to the daily questions, love letters you send as gifts, countdowns, the date you got together, and the gifts you send.
  • Settings: your language, notification choices, app icon and card theme.

2. Where It Is Stored and Who Can See It

  • Your iCloud (Apple CloudKit): Tether stores this information in your private iCloud database. When you invite your partner, the App creates a private shared space in your iCloud that only you and the partner who accepts the invite can read and write. The person who receives the invite link stores their part in the same shared space. The developer cannot access this data. Apple stores it under the iCloud Terms.
  • On Your Device: a copy is kept on your iPhone so the App and its widgets work offline. Widgets and the notification extension read it from a storage area on your device that only Tether can access.
  • Your Partner: sees your name, emoji, city, local time, weather, schedule-based status, the status you set, when you were last active, your answers (after you have both answered), your letters, gifts and shared dates. That is the purpose of the App.
  • Invite Links: anyone who has your invite link can join your shared space until a partner has joined. Share the link only with your partner. If someone else joins later, the App removes them.

3. Notifications Between Partners

When you send a touch, answer a question, set "Can talk now", send a gift or connect, the App writes a short notice to Tether's public CloudKit database so Apple can notify your partner. A notice contains only a random identifier for your partner's device, the kind of event, your first name and a time stamp. It contains no answers, letters, locations or schedules. Your device deletes the notices it sent within about 24 hours. Because the database is public, anyone who knew the random identifier could technically read these notices; the identifier is shared only with your partner and changes when you unpair.

Reminders (for example a window to talk, the daily question, countdowns, a trial ending) are scheduled on your device. We do not run our own push server.

4. Weather

To show the weather in your partner's city, the App sends that city's rounded coordinates to Apple Weather (WeatherKit). No name or other personal information is sent with it. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.

5. Personal Data We Do Not Collect

  • No Account: no sign-up, password, e-mail address or phone number.
  • No Device Location: the App does not access GPS or your precise location.
  • No Contacts or Photos: the App does not access your contacts, photo library, camera, microphone or calendar.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: no third-party ads, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Never Sold: we do not sell or rent personal information.

6. Analytics & Crash Reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the App is used and to fix bugs. They record:

  • usage events, such as screens viewed, onboarding steps, whether an invite was shared and a partner joined, that a question was answered (with the answer's length, not its text), that a touch or gift was sent, whether the paywall was shown and whether a purchase was started or completed;
  • whether you have Tether Pro, your language, whether you are connected and whether you sent or accepted the invite, a rough time-difference range (for example "6–8 h"), which widgets you added, whether notifications are allowed and whether iCloud is available;
  • crash reports and general device information (device model, iOS version, app version), with a random app instance identifier.

Analytics never receive your name, your partner's name, cities, schedules, answers, letters or dates.

7. Purchases

Tether offers an optional subscription (Tether Pro), a one-time Lifetime purchase, a Style Pack and gifts.

  • Processed by Apple: all payments take place through the Apple App Store. Your card and billing information is handled solely by Apple. We never see it.
  • RevenueCat: we use RevenueCat to verify purchases. It uses your App Store purchase records and an App User ID. Before you connect, this ID is anonymous; once you connect, both partners use the same random couple identifier, so one purchase unlocks Pro for both of you. We also share the anonymous Firebase app instance identifier with RevenueCat to measure purchases in our analytics.

Feedback: if you tap "Send feedback", your mail app opens a message to us with the App version, iOS version, device model, language and whether you use Tether Pro. Nothing is sent unless you send it. We receive your e-mail address and message and use them only to reply.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Apple (iCloud/CloudKit, Apple Weather, App Store, push notifications): Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

9. Data Retention

  • Your profile, schedule and shared content stay in iCloud and on your device until you unpair, delete them in the App, or delete them from iCloud.
  • Notices between partners are deleted within about 24 hours.
  • Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat according to their policies.

10. Unpairing and Deleting Your Data

  • Unpair (Settings → Unpair) permanently deletes the shared space, including all answers, letters, gifts and countdowns, for both partners. Your own profile and schedule stay on your device.
  • Delete all data (Settings → Delete all data) unpairs, deletes your Tether data from your iCloud and removes everything from your device. Your subscription is not cancelled; manage it in your Apple Account settings.
  • Deleting the App removes the data on your device, but data in your iCloud stays until you use "Delete all data" or remove Tether's data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

11. Data Security

iCloud data is protected by Apple's security and encryption, and only you and your partner have access to the shared space. Data on your device is protected by iOS sandboxing and device encryption. All network connections use encryption (HTTPS).

12. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 13+ and is meant for couples. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the App, please contact us.

13. Your Rights & Control

  • You can edit your profile, city, schedule and status at any time.
  • You can unpair or delete all data at any time, as described above.
  • You can turn off notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information associated with your app instance identifier, or to object to its processing. Data in your iCloud is under your control through the App and your iCloud settings. To make a request, contact us at the e-mail address below.
