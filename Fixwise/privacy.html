Fixwise Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Fixwise ("the App") is an AI home repair assistant. You take a photo of a problem in your home or describe it, and the App tells you what is likely wrong, how urgent it is, what a professional would charge, and whether it is safe to fix it yourself. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses to do that, where it goes, and how you stay in control.

1. Photos & Descriptions You Submit

Diagnosis is the core of the App. When you ask for one:

  • What Is Sent: When you ask for a diagnosis, the photos you chose (up to three, resized to a maximum of 1024 pixels), the text you typed, your answers to any follow-up questions, and the room and timing you selected are sent to an AI model to generate the answer.
  • Who Processes It: Requests are processed by Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic. Google processes this content to return the diagnosis, under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Photos Stay With You: We do not store your photos on any server. Your description and the resulting diagnosis are saved to your repair history (Section 3). We do not use your content to train our own models, and we do not sell it.
  • Only What You Choose: The App only sends photos you take or pick yourself. Please avoid including people, documents, or other personal information in the frame. The App is designed for photos of fixtures, appliances, walls, and other parts of your home.

The same applies when you ask a question in "Ask Fixwise" (the in-repair chat): your question, any photo you attach, the recent conversation, and the details of that repair are sent to Google's Gemini models to generate the answer.

2. Your Home Profile

During onboarding you may tell us your home type (house, apartment, townhouse), whether you own or rent, your DIY experience level, and the areas you are interested in. These answers personalize the advice. For example, renters are reminded to check with their landlord.

  • Your home profile is stored on your device.
  • It is included in diagnosis requests (Section 1) so the answer fits your situation. Your device's language and region settings are also included so the answer uses your language, units, and typical local costs. The App does not use GPS or your precise location.

3. Your Repair History

So you can come back to a repair, the App keeps a history of your repairs.

  • What Is Saved: For each repair: the diagnosis, your description and the room you selected, the repair guide, your progress through the steps, the tools you marked as owned, the result ("fixed", "not fixed", "hired a pro"), your estimated savings, and the text of your "Ask Fixwise" conversation about it.
  • Maintenance: When you last completed each maintenance task in the Maintain tab, and whether maintenance reminders are on.
  • Where: This history is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (part of Firebase), linked to an anonymous account that the App creates automatically. The anonymous account has no name, e-mail, or password. Security rules allow only that account to read or change its repairs.
  • Photos: The photo of each repair is kept only on your device and is never uploaded to our database. For photos attached in "Ask Fixwise", we keep only a note that a photo was sent, not the photo.
  • On Your Device: Your home profile, onboarding progress, and the number of free diagnoses used today are stored only on your device.

You can delete a single repair, or your entire repair history, in the App (Section 11).

4. Camera & Photo Library

Camera access is used only when you choose to photograph a problem or attach a photo to a question. When you pick from your photo library, the App receives only the photos you select; it cannot browse the rest of your library. Photos are used for the diagnosis (Section 1) and your repair history (Section 3) only.

Notifications: If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders on your device (for example "Did your fix work?" or a maintenance task due today). They are created locally; we do not run a push server and no reminder content is sent to us.

5. Personal Data We Do Not Collect

The App never asks you to sign up or log in. The anonymous account described in Section 3 is created automatically and contains no personal details.

  • No Personal Identifiers by Default: We do not ask for your name, phone number, or home address. Your e-mail address is collected only if you choose Sign in with Apple (see below).
  • No Location Tracking: We do not collect GPS or precise location data. When you tap "Find a pro", the App opens Apple Maps with a search such as "electrician near me". That search is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.
  • No Advertising: The App contains no third-party advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Never Sold: We do not sell or rent personal information.

Sign in with Apple (optional): If you choose to sign in, your anonymous account is linked to your Apple ID so your repair history is kept when you reinstall or change phones. Apple shares with us a stable account identifier and an e-mail address, which can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email". We use them only to sign you in and never for marketing. You can sign out or delete your account at any time in Profile → Account; deleting the account removes your repair history and maintenance progress from our database and revokes the App's access to your Apple ID.

6. Subscriptions & Payment Information

The App offers an optional auto-renewing subscription (Fixwise Premium).

  • Processed by Apple: All payments take place through the Apple App Store.
  • No Payment Details: Your card, billing, and payment information is handled solely by Apple. We never see or store it, and we do not receive your Apple ID credentials.

7. Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of services to run the App, validate purchases, protect it from abuse, and keep it stable:

  • Google Gemini via Firebase AI Logic: Generates diagnoses from the content described in Section 1.
  • Firebase App Check (with Apple App Attest): Confirms that requests come from the genuine App on a real device, so the AI service cannot be abused. It uses device attestation, not personal information.
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics & Performance Monitoring (Google): Help us understand how the App is used and find and fix bugs. They record usage events (for example, screens viewed, the category and urgency of a diagnosis, whether a purchase was started), crash reports, and performance data, along with a random app instance identifier and general device information. They do not receive your photos, your descriptions, or the contents of your repairs.
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore (Google): Create your anonymous account (or your Sign in with Apple account) and store your repair history (Section 3).
  • RevenueCat: Verifies your subscription status. It uses an anonymous App User ID and your App Store purchase records. We also share the anonymous Firebase app instance identifier with RevenueCat so subscription events can be measured in our analytics.

8. Data Retention

  • Your repair history is kept until you delete it in the App. Deleting the App does not delete it from our database, but without your anonymous account it can no longer be opened; you can ask us to delete it (Section 11).
  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the App.
  • Analytics data is kept for a limited period, no longer than 14 months.
  • Diagnosis requests sent to Google are kept according to Google's terms for the Gemini API (Section 1).

9. Data Security

Diagnosis requests and repair history are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and encrypted at rest by Google Cloud. Data on your device is protected by iOS app sandboxing and device-level encryption. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode or biometric lock.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has submitted information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Your Rights & Control

You are always in control:

  • You can revoke camera or photo access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • You can edit your home profile and delete individual repairs inside the App.
  • You can delete your entire repair history, from our database and your device, in Profile → "Delete my repair history".
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored Fixwise data, including photos, from your device.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with your app instance identifier, or to object to its processing. To make a request, contact us at the e-mail address below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
