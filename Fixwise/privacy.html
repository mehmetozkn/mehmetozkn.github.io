Fixwise Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Fixwise ("the App") is an AI home repair assistant. You take a photo of a problem in your home or describe it, and the App tells you what is likely wrong, how urgent it is, what a professional would charge, and whether it is safe to fix it yourself. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses to do that, where it goes, and how you stay in control.

1. Photos & Descriptions You Submit

Diagnosis is the core of the App, and it is the only time your content leaves your device:

  • What Is Sent: When you ask for a diagnosis, the photos you chose (up to three, resized to a maximum of 1024 pixels), the text you typed, your answers to any follow-up questions, and the room and timing you selected are sent to an AI model to generate the answer.
  • Who Processes It: Requests are processed by Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic. Google processes this content to return the diagnosis, under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • No Server of Our Own: We do not operate a server that receives or stores your photos or descriptions. We do not use them to train our own models, and we do not sell them.
  • Only What You Choose: The App only sends photos you take or pick yourself. Please avoid including people, documents, or other personal information in the frame. The App is designed for photos of fixtures, appliances, walls, and other parts of your home.

2. Your Home Profile

During onboarding you may tell us your home type (house, apartment, townhouse), whether you own or rent, your DIY experience level, and the areas you are interested in. These answers personalize the advice. For example, renters are reminded to check with their landlord.

  • Your home profile is stored on your device.
  • It is included in diagnosis requests (Section 1) so the answer fits your situation. Your device's language and region settings are also included so the answer uses your language, units, and typical local costs. The App does not use GPS or your precise location.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Repair History: Each diagnosis you receive, the first photo of it, your notes, and its status (diagnosed, in progress, fixed, hired a pro).
  • Savings Tracker: Estimated amounts you saved by fixing things yourself.
  • App Settings: Your home profile, onboarding progress, and the number of free diagnoses used today.

This data is kept in the App's local storage and is not uploaded to our servers. You can delete individual repairs in the App, and deleting the App removes all of this data from your device.

4. Camera & Photo Library

Camera access is used only when you choose to photograph a problem. When you pick from your photo library, the App receives only the photos you select; it cannot browse the rest of your library. Photos are used for the diagnosis (Section 1) and your repair history (Section 3) only.

5. Personal Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account.

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, or home address.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not collect GPS or precise location data. When you tap "Find a pro", the App opens Apple Maps with a search such as "electrician near me". That search is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.
  • No Advertising: The App contains no third-party advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Never Sold: We do not sell or rent personal information.

6. Subscriptions & Payment Information

The App offers an optional auto-renewing subscription (Fixwise Premium).

  • Processed by Apple: All payments take place through the Apple App Store.
  • No Payment Details: Your card, billing, and payment information is handled solely by Apple. We never see or store it, and we do not receive your Apple ID credentials.

7. Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of services to run the App, validate purchases, protect it from abuse, and keep it stable:

  • Google Gemini via Firebase AI Logic: Generates diagnoses from the content described in Section 1.
  • Firebase App Check (with Apple App Attest): Confirms that requests come from the genuine App on a real device, so the AI service cannot be abused. It uses device attestation, not personal information.
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics & Performance Monitoring (Google): Help us understand how the App is used and find and fix bugs. They record usage events (for example, screens viewed, the category and urgency of a diagnosis, whether a purchase was started), crash reports, and performance data, along with a random app instance identifier and general device information. They do not receive your photos, your descriptions, or your repair history.
  • RevenueCat: Verifies your subscription status. It uses an anonymous App User ID and your App Store purchase records. We also share the anonymous Firebase app instance identifier with RevenueCat so subscription events can be measured in our analytics.

8. Data Retention

  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the App.
  • Analytics data is kept for a limited period, no longer than 14 months.
  • Diagnosis requests sent to Google are kept according to Google's terms for the Gemini API (Section 1).

9. Data Security

Diagnosis requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your device is protected by iOS app sandboxing and device-level encryption. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode or biometric lock.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has submitted information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Your Rights & Control

You are always in control:

  • You can revoke camera or photo access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • You can edit your home profile and delete repairs inside the App.
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored Fixwise data from your device.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with your app instance identifier, or to object to its processing. To make a request, contact us at the e-mail address below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
