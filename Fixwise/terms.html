1. Purpose and Usage

Fixwise is an AI home repair assistant. From a photo or a description, it suggests the likely cause of a household problem and its urgency, estimates typical do-it-yourself and professional costs, and offers repair guidance and a summary you can give to a professional.

  • General Guidance Only: Diagnoses, guides, cost estimates, and chat answers are generated automatically by artificial intelligence. They may be incomplete or wrong. They are general information, not professional advice, an inspection, or a substitute for a licensed electrician, plumber, gas technician, roofer, contractor, or other qualified professional.
  • Estimates Are Not Quotes: Costs, durations, difficulty levels, and "savings" figures are rough estimates based on typical prices. Actual prices and results will vary.
  • You Decide: You are solely responsible for deciding whether to carry out a repair yourself, how you carry it out, and whether to hire a professional. When in doubt, hire a licensed professional.

2. Safety & Emergencies

Fixwise is not an emergency service. If you smell gas, see smoke, sparks or flames, notice signs of carbon monoxide, major flooding, or structural damage, or anyone is in danger, leave the area if necessary and immediately contact emergency services (911 in the United States), your gas or electric utility, or a qualified professional. Do not wait for the App.

  • The App tries to recognize dangerous situations and to recommend stopping and calling a professional. It cannot detect every hazard, and a photo or description may not show the real condition of your home.
  • Never work on gas lines, the main electrical panel, structural elements, roofs, or other high-risk systems unless you are qualified and permitted to do so.
  • Always turn off the relevant power, water, or gas supply before a repair, use appropriate protective equipment, and follow the manufacturer's instructions for tools and products.
  • Safety information in the App is always free.
