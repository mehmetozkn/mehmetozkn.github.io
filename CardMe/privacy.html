CardMe Privacy Policy

At CardMe ("the App"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle the contact information you enter and the QR codes generated from it.

1. Information You Provide

To use the App, you may enter the following types of contact information:

  • Phone number
  • Instagram username
  • X (Twitter) username
  • TikTok username
  • Other optional profile details you choose to add (e.g., name, email)

You decide which fields to fill in, and you can leave any of them empty.

2. Local Storage & On-Device Processing

CardMe follows an "On-Device First" approach:

  • Local Storage Only: The contact information you enter is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.
  • QR Code Generation: The QR code shown to the other party is generated entirely on your device. It encodes only the information you have chosen to include at that moment.
  • No Server-Side Copy: We do not keep a copy of your contact details or your generated QR codes on any server we operate.

3. Sharing With Other People

When you show your QR code to someone and they scan it, the information encoded in that QR code becomes visible to them. This is the intended purpose of the App. Please review the details in your QR code before showing it to make sure you are comfortable sharing them with that specific person.

4. Third-Party Services (Analytics Only)

We do not use third-party advertising services. We may use limited analytics services solely to improve app stability:

  • Firebase (Google): Used strictly for crash reporting (Crashlytics) and anonymous usage analytics to help us identify and fix bugs. This service does not have access to the contact information you store in the App.

5. Camera Usage

If the App offers a QR scanning feature, camera access is used only to read a QR code in real time. Camera frames are processed on your device and are not recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

6. Data Security

Since your contact information remains on your device, it is protected by the standard security protocols of your device's operating system. We recommend keeping your device protected with a passcode or biometric lock.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Your Control Over Your Data

You can view, edit, or delete the contact information stored in the App at any time through its interface. Deleting the App from your device removes this locally stored data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
