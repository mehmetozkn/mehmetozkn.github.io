1. Purpose and Usage

Flipside is a sleep planner for people who work shifts. Based on the shifts and preferences you enter, it suggests sleep times, naps, a caffeine cut-off, light exposure, and transition plans between night and day shifts, and lets you track how sleepy you feel.

  • Not Medical Advice: Flipside is a planning and sleep hygiene tool. Its suggestions are general information based on common shift-work guidance and the details you provide. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis, or treatment, and they are not a substitute for a doctor or other qualified health professional.
  • Talk to a Professional: If you have ongoing sleep problems, a sleep disorder, a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication that affects sleep or alertness, talk to a health professional before changing your sleep routine.
  • You Decide: You are responsible for deciding whether and how to follow any suggestion. Plans are based on the information you enter; if your shifts or details are wrong, the plan will be too.

2. Sleepiness, Driving & Safety

Flipside is not a safety device and is not an emergency service.

  • The sleepiness check and any suggestions before driving are a self-assessment aid. They cannot measure whether you are fit to drive, operate machinery, or perform safety-critical work.
  • Never drive or operate machinery if you feel too tired to do so safely, whatever the App shows. Use alternatives such as resting first, public transport, a taxi, or a ride with someone else.
  • Always follow your employer's fatigue, safety, and break rules. Only take a nap during a shift where your workplace allows it.
  • In an emergency, contact your local emergency number immediately.
