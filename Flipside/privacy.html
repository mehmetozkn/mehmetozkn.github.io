Flipside Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Flipside ("the App") is a sleep planner for shift workers. You enter your shifts and a few details about your sleep, and the App builds a plan that tells you when to sleep, nap, stop caffeine, and seek or avoid light. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses, where it is kept, and how you stay in control.

In short: your shifts, your plan, your sleepiness check-ins and your Apple Health sleep data stay on your device. There is no account. We use anonymous analytics and crash reports to improve the App, and Apple and RevenueCat to handle subscriptions.

1. Information You Enter

To build your plan, the App asks for:

  • Your Shifts: the days you work, the shift type (day, evening, night), start and end times, and whether a nap is possible during your break.
  • Your Sleep Profile: the kind of work you do (for example nursing or logistics), your body clock (when you wake on a free day), how much sleep you need, your commute time and how you travel to work, and your caffeine habit.
  • Sleepiness Check-ins: the sleepiness level (1–9) you choose at the start or end of a shift or before driving.
  • Plan Progress: which plan steps you marked as done or skipped.
  • Settings: your reminder choices and the language you selected.

All of this is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers. The App shares a summary of your upcoming plan with its own widgets and Live Activities through a storage area on your device that only Flipside can access.

2. Apple Health

If you are a Flipside Pro subscriber and choose to connect Apple Health, the App asks for permission to read sleep data (sleep analysis). It uses this data only to show how much you actually slept, compare it with your plan, and calculate your sleep debt.

  • The App only reads sleep data. It never writes to Apple Health and does not request any other health data type.
  • Health data is processed on your device. It is never sent to us, to analytics, or to any third party.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold or shared.
  • You can disconnect in the App (Settings → Apple Health) and revoke access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Flipside.

3. Notifications & Live Activities

If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders on your device (for example bedtime, a pre-shift nap, your last caffeine, or a break timer). Live Activities for a nap timer or a running shift are also created and updated on your device. We do not run a push server, and no reminder content is sent to us.

4. Personal Data We Do Not Collect

  • No Account: The App does not ask you to sign up or log in. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, or home address.
  • No Location: The App does not access GPS or your precise location.
  • No Photos or Contacts: The App does not access your photo library, camera, contacts, or calendar.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: The App contains no third-party advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Never Sold: We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Analytics & Crash Reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the App is used and to find and fix bugs. They record:

  • usage events, such as screens viewed, onboarding steps, whether the paywall was shown and whether a purchase was started or completed, which kind of plan step was opened or marked (for example "nap"), and that a check-in was saved (but not your sleepiness score);
  • the kind of work you selected, how you travel to work, whether you are a Pro subscriber, and the language you use;
  • crash reports and general device information (device model, iOS version, app version), along with a random app instance identifier.

Analytics do not receive your shift times, your sleep times, your sleepiness scores, or any Apple Health data.

6. Subscriptions & Payment Information

The App offers an optional auto-renewing subscription (Flipside Pro).

  • Processed by Apple: All payments take place through the Apple App Store. Your card, billing, and payment information is handled solely by Apple. We never see or store it.
  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to verify your subscription status. It uses an anonymous App User ID and your App Store purchase records. We also share the anonymous Firebase app instance identifier with RevenueCat so subscription events can be measured in our analytics.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics): Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple (App Store, Apple Health): Apple Privacy Policy

8. Data Retention

  • Data on your device (shifts, plan progress, check-ins, profile, settings) is kept until you delete it in the App or delete the App.
  • Analytics data is kept for a limited period, no longer than 14 months.
  • Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat according to their policies.

9. Data Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS app sandboxing and device-level encryption. Analytics and subscription checks are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We recommend protecting your device with a passcode or biometric lock.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults who work shifts. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the App and you want related analytics data removed, please contact us.

11. Your Rights & Control

  • You can edit your profile and shifts at any time in the App.
  • You can delete all your data from the device in Settings → "Delete all data". Your subscription is not affected.
  • You can turn off notifications and Apple Health access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored Flipside data from your device.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with your app instance identifier, or to object to its processing. To make a request, contact us at the e-mail address below.

12. Not Medical Advice

Flipside is a sleep planning and sleep hygiene tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. See our Terms of Use.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
