Hushlock Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Hushlock ("the App") is a single-player puzzle game for iPhone in which you crack combination safes by feeling the vibration and hearing the click of the dial. It is a paid app with no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses, where it goes, and how you stay in control.

1. Your Game Progress

Your progress (opened safes, stars, best times, Daily Safe results, Endless record, collectibles) and your settings (vibration strength, sound, visual assist, language) are stored only on your device. Hushlock has no account and no server of its own: we never receive or see your progress. Deleting the App deletes it.

2. Crash Reports

If the App crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) sends us a crash report so we can fix the problem. A report contains the technical state of the App at the moment of the crash (stack trace), the device model, the iOS version, the App version and a random installation identifier created by Firebase. It does not contain your name, your e-mail, your location or your game progress, and it is not used to identify you, for advertising or for tracking.

3. Remote Configuration

The App uses Firebase Remote Config (Google) to receive a few settings without an App Store update, such as the minimum supported version and the App Store link used in share messages. To deliver them, Firebase receives the random installation identifier, the App version and the device's language and country. No game data is sent.

4. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App submits your Daily Safe times, your Endless score and your achievements to Apple's Game Center. Other players can see your Game Center nickname and scores on the leaderboards. This data is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; we only see the leaderboards as any player does. You can sign out of Game Center in iPhone Settings at any time.

5. Sharing Results

When you tap Share, the App prepares a short text line (Daily Safe number, time, stars, a link to the App). It never contains the safe's combination or personal data. You choose where it goes through the iOS share sheet; we do not receive it.

6. Vibration, Sound & Permissions

Vibration and sounds are generated on your device. The App does not ask for any permission: it does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, health data or notifications.

7. Personal Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, browsing history or advertising identifier. The App contains no advertising, no analytics of how you play, and no tracking across apps or websites.

8. Purchase

Hushlock is a paid app purchased through the App Store. Payment is processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

9. Support E-mails

If you contact us through "Send feedback", your mail app opens a message to us that includes a short diagnostics line (App version, iOS version, device model, App language), which you can read and remove before sending. We use your e-mail address and message only to answer you.

10. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) - crash reports. Firebase privacy and security
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google LLC) - remote settings.
  • Game Center (Apple Inc.) - leaderboards and achievements, only if you are signed in.

11. Data Retention

  • Game progress and settings: on your device until you delete the App.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Remote Config installation identifiers: managed by Firebase and deleted when no longer used by an installation.
  • Support e-mails: kept only as long as needed to answer and follow up.

12. Data Security

Data stored by the App stays in the App's private storage on your device, protected by iOS. Crash reports and Remote Config requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to Google's infrastructure.

13. Children's Privacy

Hushlock is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: the App has no account, no chat and no user-generated content. Crash reports contain no personal information.

14. Your Rights & Control

You can delete all game data by deleting the App. You can stop Game Center submissions by signing out of Game Center. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete data about you; because we hold no personal data, such requests mostly concern crash reports, which we can delete on request if you send us the approximate date of the crash.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes, for example if a new service is added. The "Last Updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
