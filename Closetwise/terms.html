1. Purpose and Usage

Closetwise is a digital wardrobe. It builds a closet from photos of your clothes, suggests outfits for the weather and your plans, keeps a log of what you wear, plans what to pack for trips, and compares a product you are considering with what you already own (Buy Check).

  • Suggestions, Not Advice: Outfits, tags, Buy Check results ("buy", "wait", "skip"), cost-per-wear estimates, gaps and packing lists are generated automatically from your data, simple rules and, where enabled, artificial intelligence. They are suggestions for your convenience, not professional styling, financial or purchasing advice. Every buying decision is yours.
  • Estimates Can Be Wrong: Tags can be wrong, a similar piece can be missed, and cost-per-wear figures are estimates based on what you logged. Weather forecasts come from Apple Weather and can change.
  • Not Affiliated With Shops: Closetwise is not affiliated with any brand, shop or shopping app, does not sell products and contains no affiliate links. Product information comes from what you share.
  • Reminders Are a Help, Not a Guarantee: Notifications depend on your device, its settings and iOS, and may be delayed or not delivered.

3. Free Features and Closetwise Pro

  • Free: A closet with a limited number of pieces, daily outfits (a limited number of smart picks per day, then rule-based outfits), the wear log, calendar matching, the widget, the outfit canvas, a limited number of Buy Checks per month, one trip and basic insights. The current limits are shown in the App.
  • Closetwise Pro: An unlimited closet, unlimited Buy Checks and smart picks (subject to fair use below), unlimited trips, closet gaps, full insights and larger photo imports. The exact features may change as the App is updated.
  • Fair Use: To keep the AI service available for everyone, AI requests are limited per device per day and, for Pro and lifetime purchases, to a high monthly number. Above a limit, the App keeps working with on-device features and AI tagging continues later.
  • Your Data Is Never Locked: If your subscription ends, everything you added stays available; only adding beyond the free limits and Pro features are paused.
