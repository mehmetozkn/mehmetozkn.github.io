Closetwise Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Closetwise ("the App") is a digital wardrobe. You photograph your clothes, the App builds your closet, suggests outfits from it for the weather and your plans, and helps you decide before you buy something new. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses, where it is stored, what leaves your device and how you stay in control.

1. The Short Version

  • Your closet stays yours. Your photos, pieces, outfits, wear log, trips and Buy Checks are stored on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud. We have no server that stores them and we cannot see them.
  • No account. The App never asks you to sign up and does not know your name or e-mail address.
  • Optional AI tagging. Only if you turn on "Smarter tagging", cut-out photos of clothes (never your face) are sent to Google Gemini to be tagged (Section 3).
  • No ads, no tracking, never sold.

2. Your Closet: Stored on Your Device and in Your Private iCloud

  • What Is Stored: Background-removed photos of your clothes and small thumbnails; the tags of each piece (category, colours, pattern, material, season, formality, warmth, brand); the price, currency and purchase date you enter; notes; outfits you save or create; the days you wore each outfit or piece; your trips (city, dates, plans, packing list); your Buy Checks (the product image and title you shared, the price you confirmed, the result and your decision); and your preferences (the answers to the first questions, temperature unit, currency, reminder times).
  • Original Photos Are Not Kept: When you take or pick a photo, the App cuts out each piece of clothing on your device and keeps only those cut-outs. The original photo is not stored by the App.
  • Where: On your device, and in the private database of your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit) so your closet syncs between your devices. Only you, signed in with your Apple Account, can access this data; the developer cannot. If iCloud is off or full, the data stays only on your device. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Usage Counts: To apply the free limits, the App keeps a few counters (for example Buy Checks used this month) in your iCloud key-value storage. They contain no content.

3. Optional Smarter Tagging and AI

The App tags your clothes and builds outfits on your device. You can choose to let an AI model add more detailed tags. The App asks you before anything is sent, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Smarter tagging with Google Gemini.

  • If You Allow It: The App sends small (512-pixel) cut-out images of single pieces of clothing to be tagged, product images and titles you share for a Buy Check, and, for photos of you wearing clothes taken in a mirror, the photo with your face blurred on your device so the AI can find the pieces in it. For outfit suggestions on devices without on-device Apple Intelligence, it sends text only: the tags of candidate pieces, the weather and the kind of occasion (for example "work"), never the title of a calendar event.
  • Who Processes It: Google's Gemini models, through Firebase AI Logic, using the Gemini Developer API. Google may use content sent through this service, and the answers, to provide, improve and develop its products and services, and human reviewers may read it, under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. No account identifier is sent with these requests.
  • If You Choose "Keep Everything on Device": Nothing is sent to Google. Tagging, outfits and Buy Checks still work on your device.
  • On-Device Apple Intelligence: On supported iPhones the App can use Apple's on-device language model to choose and explain outfits. This happens entirely on your device.

4. Location, Weather and Calendar

  • Location: If you allow it, the App uses your approximate location only to get the weather forecast. The location is rounded and sent to Apple WeatherKit; it is not stored by us. You can choose a city instead. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.
  • Calendar: If you allow it, the App reads the titles and times of today's events (and of your trip days) on your device to match outfits to your plans, for example a meeting or a dinner. Calendar data never leaves your device and is not stored.
  • Camera and Photos: The camera is used only when you photograph clothes or a product. When you pick photos, the App receives only the photos you select and cannot see the rest of your library.

5. Buy Check, Share Extension and Widgets

  • Product Links: When you share a product link, your device fetches the page's title and preview image (like a link preview in Messages). The link is saved with your Buy Check on your device and in your iCloud; it is not sent to us. The App does not track what you shop for and contains no shopping or affiliate links.
  • Share Extension: Buy Checks made from the share sheet are computed on your device and saved in the App's private storage on your device until the App opens.
  • Widgets and Notifications: Widgets show today's outfit from a summary stored on your device. Reminders (morning outfit, evening log, waiting items, weekly summary, trips) are created on your device; we do not run a push server.

6. Analytics and Crash Reports

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics help us understand how the App is used and fix bugs. They record usage events (for example screens viewed, how many pieces were added, whether a Buy Check said buy, wait or skip, whether a purchase was started), crash reports, a random app instance identifier and general device information. They never receive your photos, brands, product names, links, notes, calendar events, city or exact prices (prices only as a broad range, such as "25–60").

7. Subscriptions and Payment Information

The App offers an optional subscription (Closetwise Pro) and a one-time lifetime purchase. All payments take place through the Apple App Store; your card and billing details are handled solely by Apple and we never see them. RevenueCat verifies your purchase using an anonymous identifier and your App Store purchase records.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit): Stores and syncs your closet in your private iCloud (Section 2).
  • Apple WeatherKit: Weather forecasts (Section 4).
  • Google Gemini via Firebase AI Logic: Optional smarter tagging (Section 3).
  • Firebase App Check (with Apple App Attest): Confirms that AI requests come from the genuine App on a real device. It uses device attestation, not personal information.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Delivers settings such as free limits without an app update.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google): Usage events and crash reports (Section 6).
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and purchase verification (Section 7).

9. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not store your closet on our own servers and cannot access it.
  • We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not show third-party ads or track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your closet data is kept on your device and in your iCloud until you delete it.
  • Delete All Data: Settings → Delete all data removes your closet from this device and from your iCloud. Your subscription is managed by Apple and is not affected.
  • Export: Settings → Export my closet gives you your photos and a list of your pieces as a file.
  • Deleting the App removes the data on that device; data in your iCloud can also be removed in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • Analytics data is kept for a limited period, no longer than 14 months.
  • Content sent to Google for tagging is kept according to Google's terms for the Gemini API (Section 3).

11. Data Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your device is protected by iOS app sandboxing and device encryption, and data in iCloud is protected by Apple. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode or biometric lock.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and is meant for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the optional AI tagging, please contact us.

13. Your Rights and Control

  • You can edit or delete any piece, outfit, log, trip or Buy Check in the App, and delete everything in Settings → Delete all data.
  • You can turn smarter tagging off at any time, and turn off camera, location, calendar and notification access in iOS Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example Türkiye under KVKK, Brazil under LGPD, Mexico, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or transfer your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because your closet is stored in your own iCloud, you can do most of this directly in the App. For anything else, contact us at the e-mail address below.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the App adds a feature that uses data differently. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
